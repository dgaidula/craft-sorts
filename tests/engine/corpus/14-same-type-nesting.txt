# A quotation inside another takes the other level from its parent, whatever mark was typed (D822).
# Only the outermost quotation keeps the level its mark asks for (" primary, ' secondary); inside it the
# levels alternate: primary, secondary, primary. A curly mark already in the text counts as the level its
# glyph has in the locale and variant (a glyph in both pairs, as in French and Dutch, counts as primary);
# a glyph that is both an opening and a closing mark (fi and sv ” and ’) is told by what is on either side
# of it (15-curly-marks-by-role.txt has those cases). The depth setting is not affected by any of this.
# es guillemets-outer has a third pair (‘ ’, the RAE order): three deep takes it (FU8).
# Expected output is written from the locale’s pairs, not copied from the engine.
