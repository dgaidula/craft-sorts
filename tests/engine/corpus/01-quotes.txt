# Straight quotes: direction, nesting, and the context around them.
# Most cases use only=quotes,apostrophes,primes (the quote family).

@ q-double-simple only=quotes,apostrophes,primes
< "Hello"
> “Hello”

@ q-double-in-sentence only=quotes,apostrophes,primes
< He said "hello" to her.
> He said “hello” to her.

@ q-double-at-start only=quotes,apostrophes,primes
< "Hello," she said.
> “Hello,” she said.

@ q-double-at-end only=quotes,apostrophes,primes
< She said, "Hello."
> She said, “Hello.”

@ q-double-question only=quotes,apostrophes,primes
< "Why?" he asked. "Because!"
> “Why?” he asked. “Because!”

@ q-two-quotations-in-one-line only=quotes,apostrophes,primes
< "One" and "two" and "three"
> “One” and “two” and “three”

@ q-nested-single-in-double only=quotes,apostrophes,primes
< "She said 'no'"
> “She said ‘no’”

@ q-nested-double-in-single only=quotes,apostrophes,primes
< 'She said "no"'
> ‘She said “no”’

@ q-nested-three-deep only=quotes,apostrophes,primes
< "He said 'she said "no"'"
> “He said ‘she said “no”’”

@ q-nested-both-closers-adjacent only=quotes,apostrophes,primes
< "He said 'go'"
> “He said ‘go’”

@ q-nested-with-punctuation only=quotes,apostrophes,primes
< "She said 'no,' and left."
> “She said ‘no,’ and left.”

@ q-single-quoted-phrase only=quotes,apostrophes,primes
< the 'best' option
> the ‘best’ option

@ q-single-quotes-with-apostrophe-inside only=quotes,apostrophes,primes
< 'I don't know,' she said.
> ‘I don’t know,’ she said.

@ q-empty-pair only=quotes,apostrophes,primes
< Say "" now
> Say “” now

@ q-quote-after-open-paren only=quotes,apostrophes,primes
< ("no")
> (“no”)

@ q-quote-before-close-paren only=quotes,apostrophes,primes
< (he said "yes")
> (he said “yes”)

@ q-quote-after-open-bracket only=quotes,apostrophes,primes
< [sic] and ["quoted"]
> [sic] and [“quoted”]

@ q-quote-after-open-brace only=quotes,apostrophes,primes
< {"key"}
> {“key”}

@ q-after-em-dash only=quotes,apostrophes,primes
< He said—"Yes"
> He said—“Yes”

@ q-before-em-dash only=quotes,apostrophes,primes
< "Yes"—he said
> “Yes”—he said

@ q-after-en-dash-spaced only=quotes,apostrophes,primes
< He said – "Yes" – and left
> He said – “Yes” – and left

@ q-after-comma-no-space only=quotes,apostrophes,primes
< He said,"Hello"
> He said,“Hello”

@ q-after-colon only=quotes,apostrophes,primes
< The sign read: "Closed"
> The sign read: “Closed”

@ q-after-ellipsis only=quotes,apostrophes,primes
< "Well…" he said
> “Well…” he said

@ q-before-ellipsis only=quotes,apostrophes,primes
< "…and then"
> “…and then”

@ q-between-slashes only=quotes,apostrophes,primes
< "yes"/"no"
> “yes”/“no”

@ q-after-exclamation only=quotes,apostrophes,primes
< "Stop!" "Go!"
> “Stop!” “Go!”

@ q-lone-straight-closer only=quotes,apostrophes,primes
< And that was it."
> And that was it.”

@ q-two-sentences-in-quote only=quotes,apostrophes,primes
< "First. Second."

@ q-quote-around-single-letter only=quotes,apostrophes,primes
< the letter "a"

@ q-quote-after-nbsp-char only=quotes,apostrophes,primes
< x\u{A0}"y"
> x\u{A0}“y”

@ q-quote-after-nbsp-entity only=quotes,apostrophes,primes

@ q-quote-after-thin-space only=quotes,apostrophes,primes

> line two”

# Quotes beside inline tags: the tags are transparent.
@ q-tag-italic-inside only=quotes,apostrophes,primes
< <i>"x"</i>
> <i>“x”</i>

@ q-tag-link-inside only=quotes,apostrophes,primes
< "<a href="/x">y</a>"
> “<a href="/x">y</a>”

@ q-tag-link-outside-then-quote only=quotes,apostrophes,primes
< He said <a href="/x">"y"</a> today
> He said <a href="/x">“y”</a> today

@ q-tag-em-closing-quote-after only=quotes,apostrophes,primes
< "He <em>did</em>" it
> “He <em>did</em>” it

@ q-tag-strong-around-opening only=quotes,apostrophes,primes
< <strong>"</strong>Hello<strong>"</strong>
> <strong>“</strong>Hello<strong>”</strong>

@ q-tag-span-with-attrs only=quotes,apostrophes,primes
< a <span class="x" title='He said "no"'>"b"</span> c
> a <span class="x" title='He said "no"'>“b”</span> c

@ q-tag-b-i-nested only=quotes,apostrophes,primes
< <b><i>"x"</i></b>
> <b><i>“x”</i></b>

@ q-tag-sup-sub-abbr-cite only=quotes,apostrophes,primes
< "<sup>1</sup>" "<sub>2</sub>" "<abbr>HTML</abbr>" "<cite>Moby</cite>"
> “<sup>1</sup>” “<sub>2</sub>” “<abbr>HTML</abbr>” “<cite>Moby</cite>”

@ q-tag-small-mark-q-u-s only=quotes,apostrophes,primes
< "<small>a</small>" "<mark>b</mark>" "<u>c</u>" "<s>d</s>"
> “<small>a</small>” “<mark>b</mark>” “<u>c</u>” “<s>d</s>”

@ q-tag-nested-quotation-across-tags only=quotes,apostrophes,primes
< "She said <i>'no'</i> twice"
> “She said <i>‘no’</i> twice”

@ q-tag-space-inside-tag-before-quote only=quotes,apostrophes,primes
< word<i> "x"</i>
> word<i> “x”</i>

@ q-tag-quote-closes-after-tag-text only=quotes,apostrophes,primes
< <i>"x</i>"
> <i>“x</i>”

@ q-tag-comment-between only=quotes,apostrophes,primes
< "a<!-- note -->b"
> “a<!-- note -->b”

# Entity names are case-sensitive: &quot; &QUOT; &apos; are quotes; &Quot; &APOS; &Apos; are not.
# The x of a hexadecimal reference may be either case.

@ q-entity-names-case-sensitive
< <p>&APOS;x&APOS; &Quot;y&Quot; &Apos;z&Apos;</p>

@ q-entity-upper-quot-is-a-quote
< <p>&QUOT;z&QUOT; and &#X22;y&#x22; and &#X27;w&#x27;</p>
> <p>“z” and “y” and ‘w’</p>

@ q-entity-wrong-case-is-not-a-prime
< <p>5&APOS;10&Quot; tall</p>
